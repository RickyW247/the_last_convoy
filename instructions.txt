THE LAST CONVOY — Player Instructions
1. Your Mission
The world has fallen to a zombie apocalypse. You lead a convoy of survivors trying to escape the horde and reach the Pacific Coast, where you can escape by ship.

Your mission is to travel west, gather supplies, keep your vehicles running, protect your survivors, and stay ahead of the approaching zombie horde.

You win by reaching the Pacific Coast alive.

You lose if the horde catches your convoy, all survivors leave, all your vehicles are lost, or your convoy's total health reaches zero.
2. Controls
Key	Action
0–5	Select a main-route destination on the map.
ENTER	Travel to the selected destination.
M	Switch between the highway map and convoy screen.
S	Scavenge for supplies at your current location.
R	Repair your vehicles using scrap.
O	Open the settings screen.
ESC	Return to the convoy screen, or quit from the main screen.
Mouse click	Select locations, click buttons, and interact with the vehicle screen.

Note: The number keys select main-route stops only. Use the mouse to select other destinations, including side stops and the Mountain Pass.
3. How to Play
Lead your convoy across the highway map towards the Pacific Coast. Choose your destinations carefully, manage your resources, and make decisions that keep your survivors alive.
- Travel: Select a destination on the map, check the route, and press ENTER or click TRAVEL to move.
- Scavenge: Press S or click SCAVENGE to search your current location for useful supplies.
- Manage resources: Keep an eye on your fuel, food, water, and scrap. Your convoy has limited storage, and supplies are essential for survival.
- Maintain vehicles: Press R or click REPAIR to restore vehicle health using scrap. Visit the VEHICLES screen to inspect, recover, or abandon vehicles.
- Watch the horde: Time passes as you travel and scavenge. Keep track of the horde's distance and danger warnings, and think carefully before taking risky routes.
- Explore: Different stops offer different opportunities. Decide which locations are worth visiting and which routes best suit your situation.
The HUD, map, minimap, and event log provide information to help you make decisions. Pay attention to warnings and adapt your strategy as the situation changes.

Your objective is simple: keep the convoy alive and reach the Pacific Coast before the horde catches you.
